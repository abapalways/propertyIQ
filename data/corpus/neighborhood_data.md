# PropertyIQ Neighborhood Safety Data

> **SYNTHETIC DATA.** The safety and crime figures below are invented for this
> project to support sample query #6 ("What's the crime rate like in this
> neighborhood?"). They are not sourced from any real crime database and must
> not be presented as official statistics. Rates are stated as incidents per
> 1,000 residents per year and as a plain comparison to a synthetic Austin
> metro baseline. Tone is factual and neutral; figures are reported without
> editorializing. See `SOURCES.md`.
>
> Synthetic Austin metro baseline (reference point for all comparisons below):
> property crime ~24 per 1,000/yr; violent crime ~4.0 per 1,000/yr.

---

## Pinecrest area (78745)

- **Property crime:** 18 per 1,000 residents/year
- **Violent crime:** 2.8 per 1,000 residents/year
- **Relative to metro baseline:** below the metro average on both measures.
- **Notes:** Primarily residential. Most reported property incidents are
  vehicle break-ins. Covered by Austin Police Department.
- **Listings in this area:** L_PINE_101, L_OAK_106, L_ELM_124.
